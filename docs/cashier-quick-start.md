# Mediescare cashier quick-start

## Start of the day

1. Sign in with your own username and password. Never use someone else's account.
2. Open **Point of sale**. If asked, count the cash in the drawer and type it in to open your shift.

## Selling

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| F2 | Find a medicine (or just scan the barcode) |
| F3 | Choose the customer |
| F4 | Change the quantity of the selected line |
| F6 | Bill discount |
| F8 | Put the bill on hold |
| F9 | Bills on hold |
| F10 | Take payment |
| Delete | Remove the selected line |
| Ctrl+N | New bill |
| Ctrl+P | Reprint the last receipt |

Your keys can be different if your manager or you changed them (My profile, then Keyboard shortcuts).

- Scan or type part of the name, the salt (for example *paracetamol*) or the code, then press **Enter**.
- Choose the unit (tablet, strip or box) and the quantity. The medicine that expires first is sold first. A batch
  close to expiry is marked.
- **F10:** type the cash received and the change appears. Card, Easypaisa, JazzCash and bank can be mixed. Wallet
  and bank payments need the transaction number.
- **On account (credit):** choose the customer first. It works only within their credit limit.

## Prescriptions

A prescription-only medicine needs the patient's and the doctor's names. A controlled medicine also needs the
patient's CNIC, the doctor's PMDC number and the prescription number. A photo of the prescription can be added.

## When the screen asks for a manager

A big discount, a changed price, credit, a large return or cancelling a bill may need a manager.
- **Manager is with you:** they type their own login on your screen.
- **Manager is elsewhere:** press **Ask a manager on another computer** and keep the window open. Your screen goes on
  by itself once they approve.

## Returns

1. Open **Sales and receipts** and find the bill (or scan the barcode on the receipt).
2. Press **Return** and choose the lines and the quantity.
3. Choose what happens to the stock: back on the shelf, damaged, expired, or quarantine.

## Cash in and out

Money put into or taken from the drawer for anything other than a sale (change from the bank, a payment to a
delivery boy) is recorded on the shift with a reason. Expenses paid from the drawer are entered under **Expenses**,
and they count in your shift automatically.

## End of the day

1. Open **Cashier shifts**, then your open shift, then **Close shift**.
2. Count the cash and type it in. If it does not match, write what happened.
3. Print the Z report if your manager wants it.

## Good to know

- No internet? Keep selling. Everything is saved on this computer and shared when the network is back.
- The screen signs out after a while if nobody uses it. Sign in again and your work is still there.
- If a message appears, read it. It says what to do.
